# Public Sources

This repository is primarily an original methodology notebook. The two case-study backgrounds are tied to official UESTC / Glasgow College program coverage.

## Tape-out program coverage

1. **格拉斯哥孊院首次开设芯片流片课程并圆满结课**  
   Glasgow College, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.  
   https://www.gla.uestc.edu.cn/info/1003/17178.htm

2. **格拉斯哥学院持续推进本科生芯片流片实践教学 57名学生完成真实工程流片**  
   UESTC News, 2026-06-26.  
   https://news.uestc.edu.cn/info/1004/41344.htm

The 2026 report publicly describes the full flow from design and simulation through layout, tape-out data review, wafer fabrication, packaging and testing. It also describes the advanced LDO project as a 3.3 V → 1.8 V regulator using a reference, error amplifier, comparator and power MOS stage.

## Technical terminology

DRC, LVS, PEX, PVT, Monte Carlo, post-layout simulation and hierarchy/config-view concepts are discussed here at a process-independent engineering level. Exact commands, rule decks, extraction settings and signoff criteria must come from authorized tool and foundry documentation for the target process.
